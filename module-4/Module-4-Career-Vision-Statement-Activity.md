# Career Vision Statement Activity

This activity guides you through a structured writing process to develop a meaningful Career Vision Statement. You will build it in layers — starting with raw material from your previous work, developing a draft, refining it, and then committing to a final version.

---

## Step 1 — Gather Your Raw Material

Before writing, pull together the following from your previous work:

- Your Personal Empathy Map insights
- Your SWOT Analysis
- Your Personal POV Statement
- Your RIASEC Profile
- Your Career Exploration Map

---

## Step 2 — Answer the Foundation Questions

**What kind of work do I want to do?**
*(Think about tasks, not job titles. What activities do you want to fill your professional days with? What problems do you want to work on?)*
[I want to work on protecting digital networks and secure systems from cyber threats. My focus is on configuring multi-factor authentication, enforcing strict access controls, and making sure organizations follow proper security policies like PCI DSS standards. I also enjoy using video editing tools to create engaging content that simplifies complex security concepts for ordinary people.]

**Who do I want to serve or impact?**
*(Who are the people, communities, or sectors your work will benefit? Be as specific as you can.)*
[I want to impact small businesses, everyday users, and educational organizations that are vulnerable to cyber attacks. A lot of small enterprises and students do not have massive IT security teams, so I want to build systems and policies that keep their sensitive data safe without making things overly complicated for them.]

**What values do I want my career to embody?**
[Integrity, accountability, continuous improvement, and practical problem-solving.]

**What kind of professional do I want to become?**
*(What qualities, character traits, or reputation do you want to be known for in your field?)*
[I want to be known as a reliable, highly skilled cybersecurity professional who stays calm under pressure. I want a reputation for combining technical expertise with strong communication skills, someone who can fix complex network issues and still explain what went wrong in plain, simple terms.]

**What does success look like for me — not financially, but meaningfully?**
*(Set salary aside for a moment. What would make you feel that your career was truly worthwhile?)*
[Success means building a career where I wake up genuinely interested in the problems I get to solve each day. It means knowing that the systems I protect keep real people from having their personal information stolen, while having the flexibility to keep growing as an athlete and creative person.]

---

## Step 3 — Draft Your Career Vision Statement

Using your foundation questions as raw material, write a first draft of your Career Vision Statement. Aim for 3–5 sentences. Use the structure below as a guide — but feel free to depart from it once you have a draft:

> "My career vision is to [configure secure networks] for/with [businesses and organization], so that [everyday users can rely on digital systems without worrying about security breaches]. I want to be known as a professional who approaches complex technical challenges with integrity, clear communication, and steady problem-solving. I see myself building a career in [cybersecurity field] where I can strengthen identity access controls and protect user data, grounded in my commitment to continuous learning and accountability.

**My Draft Career Vision Statement:**
[My career vision is to design secure digital infrastructure and use cybersecurity standards for organizations, protecting  data from evolving threats. I want to be known as a specialist at  technical safeguards and practical, real-world execution.]

---

## Step 4 — Refine and Finalize

Read your draft aloud (or read it slowly in your head). Ask yourself:

- Does this sound like me, or does it sound like what I think I should say?
- Is it specific enough to be meaningful?
- Is it broad enough to allow for growth?
- Does it excite me when I read it?

Revise as needed and write your final version below.

**My Final Career Vision Statement:**

> [My career vision is to work in cybersecurity protecting networks and keeping data safe for everyday organizations. I want to be known as a reliable tech guy who can handle big security problems and explain them in simple terms. I see myself growing in the IT field where I can help stop cyber threats while staying true to my values of hard work, discipline, and getting better every day.]

---

💡 **Note:** Your Career Vision Statement is a living document. You are not locked into it forever. You will revisit and revise it in Module 5 and beyond as your self-knowledge deepens and your career exploration continues.
