# SMART Goals Activity

Vague intentions do not produce results. This activity moves you from intention to commitment — from "I hope to" into "I will, by when, measured how." You will write a minimum of 5 SMART Goals across academic, career, and personal development categories.

---

## Step 1 — Review Your POV Statement

Reread your POV Statement. Your goals should move you in the direction defined by your POV Statement. If they do not connect, something is misaligned.

**My POV Statement (for reference):**
[I am a college sprinter studying Information Science who is focused on going pro in track while building a real backup plan in tech. I need an education that gives me hands-on skills in database systems, data analytics, and time management. Because I realized that tech and sports actually work together, and having a strong degree gives me the confidence to shoot for my highest goals on and off the track.]

---

## Step 2 — Brainstorm Goal Areas

Before writing formal SMART goals, brainstorm freely. For each category below, list 3–4 things you want to accomplish in the next 1–4 years. Do not filter yourself in this step — just generate ideas.

**Academic** *(courses, GPA, relationships with faculty, study habits...)*
-Keep a GPA of 3.2 or higher every semester while balancing track travel and classes.
-Master SQL and database design in my core Information Science courses.
-Meet with my academic advisor twice a semester to make sure my schedule fits my track training.
-Build strong relationships with my Information Science professors during office hours.

**Career** *(internships, skills, certifications, networking...)*
-Earn a beginner tech certification like CompTIA Security+ or AWS Cloud Practitioner.
-Secure a summer IT or operations internship with a major airline (like Delta or United).
-Build a portfolio project using sports data analytics to track my own sprint times and biomechanics.
-Set up a clean LinkedIn profile to connect with tech recruiters and sports management contacts.


**Personal Development** *(campus involvement, health, community, leadership...)*
-Maintain a strict sleep, recovery, and nutrition routine to stay healthy for track season.
-Improve my daily time management between class, weight training, track practice, and homework.
-Take on a leadership role among student-athletes on my track team.
-Build a clean personal brand on social media to attract athletic sponsors and business contacts.

## Step 3 — Select and Write 5 SMART Goals

Choose at least 5 goals from your brainstorm — at minimum, include:


S - I will earn at least a 3.2 GPA this semester by attending every class, turning in all Information Science assignments on time, and studying at least 6 hours every week during open blocks between track practices.
M — Measurable: How will I track progress? How will I know I succeeded?
I will check my grades on Canvas every Sunday night to make sure no class falls below a B, and my official final semester report card will show a GPA of 3.2 or higher.
A — Achievable: Why is this realistic given my current situation?
It is realistic because I already have strong daily discipline from track training, and I can use study hours during travel and downtime between practices.
R — Relevant: How does this connect to my POV Statement and career direction?
Keeping a solid GPA keeps me eligible to compete in college track while keeping my academic foundation strong for a tech career.
T — Time-Bound: By when will I achieve this? What is my deadline?
By the end of this current academic semester.
